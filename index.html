About

I've liked building things for as long as I can remember. It started with marble tracks that were far taller than they needed to be, Rube Goldberg machines and contraptions in Minecraft. Now it's robot arms and gearboxes.

I want to understand how the things around me work, and I'm happiest when I own a design from the first sketch to the part running on the bench. Over five internships that has meant mechanisms, PCBAs, firmware and a lot of time with vendors working out how a part will get made.
